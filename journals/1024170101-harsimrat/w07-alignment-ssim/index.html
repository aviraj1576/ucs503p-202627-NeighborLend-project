    Week 7 : Prototype: Aligning Two Photos and Comparing Them with SSIM

    Problem

    For the prototype, compare a handover photo and a return photo of the same item and mark regions that may have changed, without marking the normal differences listed in Week 5 (angle, lighting, background).

    Relevant Context

    Both photos have passed the quality gate (Week 6), and each has a mask of the object. The two photos are never taken from exactly the same position.

    Key Observation

    Before two photos can be compared pixel by pixel, the "before" photo must be warped so that each point of the object lands where it is in the "after" photo. This needs reliable point correspondences, and a single wrong match can spoil the transform unless the estimate is robust to outliers.

    Solution

    1. Local features (SIFT) on the object only, with contrast-limited histogram equalisation (CLAHE) to reduce lighting differences, and RootSIFT normalisation of the descriptors:

    detector = cv2.SIFT_create(nfeatures=1200, enable_precise_upscale=True)
gray = cv2.createCLAHE(clipLimit=2.0, tileGridSize=(8, 8)).apply(gray)
keypoints, descriptors = detector.detectAndCompute(gray, mask.astype(np.uint8) * 255)
descriptors = np.sqrt(descriptors / np.maximum(descriptors.sum(axis=1, keepdims=True), 1e-8))

    2. Mutual matching with Lowe's ratio test: a match is kept only if it passes the 0.75 ratio test in both directions (before → after and after → before).

    3. RANSAC homography, with a fallback to a simpler similarity transform under the same checks. At least 12 inliers and an inlier ratio of 0.45 are required; otherwise the pair is reported as weak_geometric_support_or_view_change.

    matrix, inliers = cv2.findHomography(src, dst, cv2.RANSAC, 3.0)

    4. SSIM over the aligned overlap. A global gain and offset correct the overall brightness first; then a Gaussian-window SSIM map is computed, and a pixel is a change candidate only if both the SSIM difference and the absolute grey difference are large. Connected regions are kept if their area is within limits, and the 20 largest are returned:

    difference = np.clip((1 - ssim_map(adjusted, gray2)) / 2, 0, 1)
proposals = (difference > config.difference_threshold) & (np.abs(adjusted - gray2) > 0.06) & overlap

    A morphological closing (not an opening) joins broken pieces, because an opening would erase the one- or two-pixel-wide lines that thin scratches and cracks produce.

    Because

    RANSAC fits the transform to random small subsets and keeps the one most points agree with, so wrong matches do not pull it away. SSIM compares local mean, contrast and structure, so a uniform lighting change affects it far less than a scratch does. Every step can stop the pipeline with a named reason, and the output is a set of candidates for a person to review.
